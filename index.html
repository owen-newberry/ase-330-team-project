Welcome to Quest Log! 🎯
📋
Create Your First Board

Boards are where you organize your tasks. Think of them as project workspaces where you can manage everything in one place.

  • ✓ Click "Boards" in the navigation
  • ✓ Then click "+ Add Board"
  • ✓ Give it a name and start organizing!
📝
Add & Manage Tasks

Each board has three columns: To Do, In Progress, and Done.

  • ✓ Click "+ Add Task" to create tasks
  • ✓ Set due dates, priority, and point values
  • ✓ Drag tasks between columns as you progress
  • ✓ Click a task to view details or edit
⭐
Earn Points & Rewards

Complete tasks to earn points and redeem them for rewards!

  • ✓ Move tasks to "Done" column
  • ✓ Click "Redeem" to claim your points
  • ✓ Set personal goals in the Rewards page
  • ✓ Claim rewards when you reach your goal!
🏆
Unlock Achievements

Earn badges as you use Quest Log and level up your productivity!

  • 🥉 Bronze: Complete basic milestones
  • 🥈 Silver: Show consistent progress
  • 🥇 Gold: Master-level achievements
  • 🔥 Streaks: Complete tasks daily to build your streak!
🚀
You're All Set!

You now know the basics of Quest Log. Explore these features:

  • 📊 Dashboard: View all your boards and upcoming tasks
  • 📅 Calendar: See tasks by due date
  • 🏆 Achievements: Track your progress and badges
  • 🎁 Rewards: Set goals and claim rewards
