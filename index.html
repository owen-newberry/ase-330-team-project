Welcome to Quest Log! 🎯
📋
Create Your First Board

Boards are where you organize your tasks. Think of them as project workspaces where you can manage everything in one place.

  • ✓ Click "Boards" in the navigation
  • ✓ Then click "+ New board"
  • ✓ Give it a name and choose a background
📝
Add Tasks to Your Board

Each board has three columns: To Do, In Progress, and Done.

  • ✓ Add tasks by typing in the input field
  • ✓ Drag tasks between columns as you work
  • ✓ Add descriptions to keep track of details
🏆
Earn Points & Rewards

Complete tasks to earn points and redeem them for rewards!

  • ✓ Move tasks to "Done" column
  • ✓ Click "Redeem" button to earn points
  • ✓ Set goals in the Rewards page
  • ✓ Track your progress toward your goals
🚀
You're All Set!

You now know the basics of Quest Log. Here are some tips to get started:

  • 📊 Dashboard: View all your boards and tasks
  • 🔍 Search: Quickly find tasks and boards
  • 👤 Account: Manage your profile and settings
  • 🎁 Rewards: Set goals and track points
Tip: You can restart this tutorial anytime from your Account page.
