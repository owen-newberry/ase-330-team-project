## 📋 Usage Guide

### Creating Tasks with Priority
1. Open a board
2. In any column, fill in task title
3. Add optional description
4. **Select priority** from dropdown (Low/Medium/High/Urgent)
5. **Add due date** (optional)
6. Click "Add" to create task

### Sorting Tasks
- Click **"Sort by Priority"** button to organize by urgency
- Click **"Sort by Date"** button to organize by due date
- Sorting is per-column (To Do, In Progress, Done)

### Earning Points & Badges
1. Complete tasks by moving them to "Done"
2. Click **"Redeem +10 pts"** button on completed tasks
3. Earn XP and points
4. Unlock badges automatically
5. Watch for confetti celebrations! 🎉

### Creating Colored Boards
1. Click "**+ New board**"
2. Choose a **color or gradient** from 8 options
3. Preview shows your selection
4. Board will display with colored top bar
