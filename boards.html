Managing Your Boards

Boards are your project workspaces. Each board contains tasks organized in columns.

  • ➕ Create a Board: Click "+ Add Board" and give it a name
  • 👁️ Open a Board: Click on any board card to view its tasks
  • ✏️ Rename/Delete: Click the 3-dot menu on a board card
  • 📊 Track Progress: See task counts for each board
Tip: Create separate boards for different projects to stay organized!
