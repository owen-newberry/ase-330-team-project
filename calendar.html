📅 Calendar Guide

Using the Calendar

See all your tasks organized by due date in one view!

  • 📆 Navigate: Use Previous/Next buttons to browse months
  • 🟢 Today: Click "Today" to jump to current date
  • 🎯 Click Tasks: Click any task to go to its board
Priority Colors:
  • 🔴 Urgent: Red background - needs immediate attention
  • 🟠 High: Orange background - important tasks
  • 🟡 Medium: Yellow background - normal priority
  • 🟢 Low: Green background - can wait
Tip: Add due dates to your tasks to see them on the calendar!
